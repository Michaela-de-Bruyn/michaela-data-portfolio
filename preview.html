01 — ABOUT ME

My career path?
More like a scenic route.

IT→DATA→ENGINEERING

Apparently, my career forgot to follow a straight line. On paper, it might look a little unexpected: IT → Data Analysis → BI → Business Analysis → Scrum Master → Data Engineering. But when I look at it as a whole, every role added another piece to the puzzle.

I’ve built technical skills, worked with data, understood business problems, supported teams and processes, worked with stakeholders and spent a lot of time sitting between the technical and business sides of technology. Over time, I realised I wanted to move closer to the technical side again — to understand what happens behind the scenes and build the systems that make data useful.

That’s what led me to Data Engineering. The deeper I go into SQL, databases, ETL, data pipelines, data modelling and cloud platforms, the more curious I become about how all the pieces fit together. I’m naturally curious, eager to learn and usually the person asking, “Okay… but how does this actually work?”

I also see my mix of technical and non-technical experience as an advantage. I understand the data and technology, but I also understand business problems, communication and the people who need the solution. I enjoy bridging that gap — translating business needs into technical thinking and making technical concepts easier to understand.

So, no — I’m not starting over. I’m building on everything I’ve already learned and adding a new technical layer. Right now, my focus is becoming a strong Data Engineer, getting hands-on, continuing to learn and seeing where my curiosity takes me next. Apparently, the scenic route can still get you where you’re going.

THE SHORT VERSIONTechnical enough to build. Business-minded enough to understand the why. Curious enough to keep asking how.
